## ⚡ Instantly Explore TestBay

TestBay is a **lightweight test result management platform** —
designed to help you **track, analyze, and present** your testing data with zero setup.

This standalone version runs entirely in your browser.
No sign-up. No servers. Just open and explore.

---

### ✅ What You Can Do

- 📊 **View Your Dashboard** – Pass/fail rates, trends, and key metrics
- 📁 **Create Projects** – Separate frameworks, clients, or environments
- 📂 **Upload Test Results** – `.txt`, `.log`, `.xml`, `.json`, or paste raw output
- 📈 **Analyze Trends** – Compare frameworks and spot performance changes
- 🧹 **Start Fresh Anytime** – Clear all demo data in one click

---

### 🔍 Why This Demo?

- **Zero Install** – Opens instantly in any modern browser
- **Completely Private** – Uses `localStorage` only
- **Fast & Responsive** – Works great on desktop and mobile
- **Perfect for Previews** – Ideal for product tours and sales calls
